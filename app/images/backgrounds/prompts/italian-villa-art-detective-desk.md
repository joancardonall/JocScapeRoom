# Prompt: vil.la italiana artistica d'investigacio

## Objectiu

Crear una imatge de fons 16:9 per al joc, amb una estetica italiana mes lluminosa, felic, artistica i mediterrania. L'escena ha de semblar una vil.la costanera o toscana reconvertida en estudi d'investigacio patrimonial: art antic, disseny elegant, arquitectura historica, llum calida i una sensacio de bellesa amb misteri subtil.

La composicio ha de funcionar com a interfície point-and-click: els objectes han de ser grans, visibles, separats i prou nets per col.locar-hi hotspots invisibles.

## Prompt principal

```text
A bright artistic interactive detective game background set inside an elegant Italian coastal villa investigation studio, inspired by Tuscany, Mediterranean beach houses, antique heritage, art restoration, and refined Italian interior design. The scene is warm, happy, luminous, and sophisticated, with a subtle mystery beneath the beauty. A sunlit loggia or open study with pale limestone walls, terracotta floor tiles, travertine desk surface, soft arches, handmade ceramic details, olive green accents, white linen curtains, bougainvillea outside, cypress trees, and a partial view of the sea or a warm Tuscan garden in the background.

The image must be designed as a playable point-and-click investigation interface. Include five large, clearly separated clickable objects integrated naturally into the scene: a slim modern laptop or desktop screen on the left side of the desk, an elegant evidence board or glass investigation panel in the back center with photos and art conservation notes but no readable text, a large cream or warm ochre case folder in the right foreground, a low drawer or organized document tray in the lower left foreground, and a discreet transparent forensic evidence bag or evidence box in the lower right foreground containing an art-related clue such as a ceramic fragment, restoration glove, pigment sample, antique key, or sealed document fragment.

Use a frontal slightly top-down camera angle, close enough that the investigation objects occupy most of the image while still leaving clear separation between them. The atmosphere should feel like a beautiful Italian art mystery: cheerful Mediterranean sunlight, elegant shadows, heritage materials, tasteful design, fresh coastal air, refined vacation-house mood, art and antiquity. Keep the architecture present but secondary to the clickable objects. 16:9 composition, 1920x1080, polished cinematic realism, premium hidden-object game background, clear readable silhouettes, balanced composition, no clutter.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
dark noir office, gothic mansion, cyberpunk neon, cold police station, medieval fantasy, horror atmosphere, gloomy basement, messy clutter, tiny objects, distant objects, huge empty room, wide real estate photo, unreadable tiny props, readable text, logos, people, faces, hands, weapons in focus, gore, blood, chaotic papers, distorted laptop, distorted perspective, fisheye lens, cropped important objects, overexposed white walls, blurry evidence board, duplicate computers, duplicate folders, floating objects, plastic-looking props, cartoon style
```

## Variant mes propera

```text
Make the camera closer, like a premium investigation desktop interface inside an Italian villa. The desk and the five investigation objects should fill most of the frame. The sea view, arches, ceramics, plants, and Tuscan materials should frame the scene without making the room feel empty or distracting from the clickable objects.
```

## Notes de seleccio

Tria una imatge on:

- l'ordinador, el panell, la carpeta, el calaix i la bossa de proves siguin immediatament identificables
- l'ambient sigui lluminos, mediterrani i artistic, no fosc ni policial
- la vil.la italiana es noti per materials, llum, arcs, terracota, plantes i vista exterior
- els objectes siguin prou grans per funcionar com a hotspots
- no hi hagi text llegible generat dins la imatge
- la composicio sembli una interfície de joc, no una fotografia immobiliaria
- el fons sigui bonic pero no robi protagonisme als elements del cas
