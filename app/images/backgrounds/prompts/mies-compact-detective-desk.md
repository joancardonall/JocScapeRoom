# Prompt: taula detective compacta inspirada en Mies

## Objectiu

Crear una imatge de fons 16:9 mes propera i compacta que la versio de pavello obert. L'estil continua inspirat en Mies van der Rohe, pero la composicio ha de funcionar millor com a interfície de joc: objectes grans, propers, clars i clicables.

La imatge no ha de semblar buida ni massa espaiosa. Ha de sentir-se com una taula d'investigacio elegant dins d'un interior modernista.

## Prompt principal

```text
An elegant close-up interactive detective game background set on a refined investigation desk inside a modernist interior inspired by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, not a direct copy of any real building. The camera is much closer to the desk, with a compact composition and clearly visible objects in the foreground and middle ground. Sophisticated minimalism, strong horizontal lines, precise geometry, travertine surface, polished steel details, smoked glass, subtle green marble or warm onyx accents, soft reflections, tasteful architectural atmosphere.

The scene should feel intimate, designed, and playable, not spacious or empty. Include five large, clearly separated clickable objects: a thin modern laptop or monitor on the left side, an elegant smoked-glass evidence board or vertical investigation panel in the back center, a neat black or cream case folder in the right foreground, a low minimalist drawer or document tray in the lower left foreground, and a discreet transparent forensic evidence bag or evidence box in the lower right foreground.

The objects should occupy most of the image, arranged like a beautiful point-and-click game interface. Use a frontal slightly top-down camera angle, 16:9 composition, 1920x1080. Keep the background architectural but secondary: glass wall, slim steel column, travertine or marble planes, soft water reflection outside. No large empty hall, no distant wide pavilion view. Warm elegant evening light, refined mystery, artistic gift quality, minimal but not empty.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
wide empty room, huge open hall, distant objects, tiny objects, too much empty floor, sparse composition, readable text, letters, logos, people, faces, messy clutter, cyberpunk neon, gothic style, rustic wood, old detective office cliches, extreme darkness, blurry objects, cropped important objects, distorted perspective, fisheye lens, chaotic papers, gore, blood, weapons in focus
```

## Variant encara mes propera

```text
Make the camera even closer, like a premium desktop interface. The investigation objects should fill the frame while still leaving enough separation for invisible clickable hotspots. The architectural background should only appear as elegant materials and reflections, not as a large room.
```

## Notes de seleccio

Tria una imatge on:

- els objectes siguin prou grans per clicar-los
- la carpeta, ordinador, panell, calaix i bossa siguin identificables
- l'escena no sembli buida
- el fons tingui estil Mies sense robar protagonisme
- no hi hagi text inventat
- la composicio sembli una interfície, no una fotografia immobiliaria
