# Prompt: habitacio d'estudi detective inspirada en Mies

## Objectiu

Crear una habitacio coherent, elegant i jugable: un estudi privat de detectiu amb un escriptori integrat en una arquitectura inspirada en Mies van der Rohe. L'escena ha de semblar dissenyada com un espai real, no com una col.leccio d'objectes posats a sobre d'un pavello.

L'estil ha de mantenir minimalisme, proporcio, vidre, acer, traverti, marbre i calidesa discreta, pero amb mes sensacio d'habitacio i escriptori.

## Prompt principal

```text
An elegant private detective study room designed with a refined modernist architectural language inspired by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, not a direct copy of any real building. The room has a beautiful integrated desk as the natural center of the composition, built into the space with travertine, dark wood veneer, smoked glass, polished steel, and subtle green marble or warm onyx accents. The design feels coherent, intentional, and lived-in, like a sophisticated architectural interior made for investigation work.

The scene is an intimate room, not a huge open pavilion. Include floor-to-ceiling glass on one side, slim steel columns, horizontal architectural lines, stone surfaces, a low cabinet, warm indirect lighting, calm reflections, and a quiet evening atmosphere. The desk should feel custom-designed for the room, with investigation materials naturally arranged on it and around it.

Make the interactive objects feel integrated into the room: a slim laptop or desktop screen placed naturally on the desk, a refined evidence wall or glass pinboard behind or beside the desk, a case folder lying naturally on the desk surface, a drawer or low cabinet built into the desk area, and a discreet forensic evidence box or transparent evidence bag placed on a side shelf or lower desk corner. They should not look like separate props; they should belong to the interior design.

Camera angle: frontal with a slight top-down view, medium-close interior composition, 16:9, 1920x1080. The desk and surrounding investigation area should fill much of the frame, while still showing the architectural room around it. Balanced, tasteful, artistic, warm, minimal but not empty, mysterious but elegant. No readable text, no logos, no people.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
objects randomly placed, props pasted onto scene, floating objects, wide empty pavilion, huge empty hall, distant desk, tiny objects, showroom with no function, generic office cubicle, messy clutter, old detective office cliches, readable text, letters, logos, people, faces, cyberpunk neon, gothic style, rustic cabin, extreme darkness, blurry objects, distorted perspective, fisheye lens, gore, blood, weapons in focus
```

## Variant mes acollidora

```text
Make the room feel warmer and more personal, like a quiet private study designed as a gift: soft amber lighting, elegant shadows, rich but restrained materials, calm reflections, and a subtle romantic atmosphere while keeping the Mies van der Rohe inspired modernist precision.
```

## Variant mes arquitectonica

```text
Emphasize architectural coherence: the desk, cabinet, evidence wall, and storage should feel custom-built into the room using the same material palette of travertine, smoked glass, steel, dark wood, and marble. The investigation elements should look designed as part of the architecture.
```

## Notes de seleccio

Tria una imatge on:

- sembli una habitacio real amb escriptori, no una sala buida
- els objectes d'investigacio estiguin integrats
- el desk sigui protagonista
- hi hagi prou espai visual per identificar els hotspots
- l'estil sigui elegant, arquitectonic i amb bon gust
- no aparegui text inventat
