# Prompt: escena detective inspirada en Mies van der Rohe

## Objectiu

Crear una imatge de fons 16:9 per al joc, amb una estetica arquitectonica elegant inspirada en Mies van der Rohe: minimalisme, proporcio, vidre, acer, traverti, marbre, llum suau i espai obert.

La imatge ha de ser bonica, sofisticada i jugable: els objectes clicables han de quedar visibles i separats per poder posar-hi hotspots invisibles.

## Prompt principal

```text
An elegant interactive detective game background set inside a modernist glass pavilion inspired by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, not a direct copy of any real building. A refined architectural interior with strong horizontal lines, open plan composition, floor-to-ceiling glass walls, slim steel columns, travertine floor slabs, subtle green marble and warm onyx accents, calm reflections from a shallow water pool outside, soft natural evening light, sophisticated minimalism, museum-like atmosphere, tasteful and intimate.

The scene is designed as a playable point-and-click investigation interface. Include five clearly visible clickable objects, separated from each other: a thin modern laptop on a glass or stone desk on the left, an elegant evidence board or smoked glass investigation panel in the background center, a neat cream or black case folder on a travertine surface in the right foreground, a low minimalist drawer cabinet on the lower left, and a discreet transparent forensic evidence bag or evidence box on the lower right.

Camera angle: frontal with a slight top-down view, wide 16:9 composition, 1920x1080. Clean balanced layout, enough empty space between objects, no clutter, no people, no readable text, no logos, no heavy shadows. The image should feel like a beautiful architectural gift, artistic, minimal, precise, warm, and mysterious.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
no readable text, no letters, no logos, no people, no faces, no messy clutter, no cyberpunk neon, no gothic style, no rustic wood cabin, no old detective office cliches, no extreme darkness, no blurry objects, no cropped important objects, no distorted perspective, no fisheye lens, no chaotic papers, no exaggerated crime scene gore, no blood, no weapons in focus
```

## Variant mes calida

```text
Make the atmosphere warmer and more romantic, with golden evening light, subtle reflections on marble and glass, a refined intimate mood, elegant shadows, and a quiet personal gift feeling, while keeping the minimal Mies van der Rohe inspired architectural language.
```

## Variant mes museistica

```text
Make the atmosphere cooler, more gallery-like and architectural, with precise geometry, calm white light, sharper reflections, polished steel, travertine, green marble, and a highly composed modernist exhibition quality.
```

## Notes per revisar la imatge

Abans de triar la imatge final, comprova:

- que sigui clarament 16:9
- que els cinc objectes siguin visibles
- que cap objecte important quedi tallat
- que hi hagi prou separacio per posar hotspots
- que no hi hagi text inventat o lletres rares
- que l'estil sigui elegant i arquitectonic, no massa carregat
- que la imatge no sembli una copia literal del Pavello de Barcelona o la Farnsworth House
